Before you play

Can I start as a complete beginner?

Yes. Choose Beginner mode to start with the basics. A1 and A2 modes are also available if you already know some of the language.

Do I need a subscription?

Desktop editions are one-time purchases. Android offers monthly or permanent access. The Premium Lifetime Pass covers desktop and mobile. See editions & prices.

Can I continue after the demo?

You can carry your demo progress into the full game on the same platform. Find the demo on Steam.
