WONDERLANG / Privacy policy

Privacy policy

This policy describes the information used by WonderLang’s website, account, purchase and cloud-save services. For privacy requests, contact contact@wonderlang.net.

Updated 3 October 2026

01

Accounts, orders and cloud saves

We use your sign-in identity and email to authenticate you, connect purchases to your account and provide support. Purchase records include the product, amount, currency and transaction identifiers. Stripe or the relevant app store processes payments.

When you use eligible cloud-save features, your profiles and save files are stored to synchronize progress across devices. These services also use technical and security logs to operate and investigate problems.

02

Newsletters and messages

If you subscribe, your email is processed through MailerLite to send WonderLang news and offers. You can unsubscribe using the link in a newsletter. Newsletter subscriptions are separate from necessary order confirmations and account emails.

When you contact us, we use the information you provide to respond and resolve your request.

03

Measurement and advertising

With the applicable permissions, we use website measurement and advertising tools from Meta, TikTok and Google. These may receive browser or device identifiers, ad-click identifiers, IP address, user agent and purchase details to measure advertising performance. Some reporting uses hashed email addresses for matching; hashing does not make this data anonymous.

The test website offers optional conversion experiments. If you accept measurement, a visitor identifier and experiment assignment connect visits and interactions with checkout results. Preview visits are excluded from experiments. You can change your test-site measurement choice through the privacy controls.

04

Service providers

Providers supporting these services include Netlify for hosting, Google/Firebase for authentication and cloud data, Stripe and app stores for payments, Google email services for account/order communications, MailerLite for newsletters, and enabled advertising platforms for measurement. We share the information needed for the relevant service; these providers may process data outside your country.

05

Your choices and rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your personal data, withdraw consent, or raise a privacy concern at contact@wonderlang.net. You may also complain to the relevant data-protection authority. Unsubscribing from marketing does not prevent necessary account and purchase messages.

Data needed to provide access, maintain purchase records, prevent abuse or meet legal obligations may need to be retained. Contact us for questions about a particular record or retention period.

06

Security and updates

We use access controls and other safeguards to protect data. No online transmission or storage system is completely secure. This policy may be updated when the services or practices change.
