1. WHO WE ARE AND WHAT THIS POLICY COVERS

WonderLang is operated by Jonathan Bariller, trading as WonderLang, France. Jonathan Bariller is the controller responsible for the personal data described in this policy.

Privacy contact: contact@wonderlang.app

This policy covers the WonderLang iOS and Android apps and the WonderLang account, purchase-verification, cloud-save, and support services used with them. If you connect the same WonderLang account on another supported platform, your account, access rights, and cloud saves can be used across those platforms, subject to your purchases and available features.

Apple, Google, payment providers, and sign-in providers also process information under their own terms and privacy notices. Visiting our marketing websites or subscribing to a newsletter can involve additional processing described in the notices provided on those pages. This app policy does not replace a website's cookie choices or newsletter notice.

2. INFORMATION WE PROCESS

Account and sign-in information

If you create or sign in to a WonderLang account, we process your email address, WonderLang account identifier, linked sign-in providers and their identifiers, and any name or profile information supplied by your chosen provider. Sign-in methods can include Google, Apple, and an email sign-in link, depending on the platform. Apple may provide a private relay email address. We process authentication and session information to keep you signed in and secure your account. We do not receive your Google or Apple password.

Purchases and subscriptions

We process the store or purchase provider, product purchased, transaction and subscription references, purchase-verification information, purchase and renewal dates, access period, and subscription, cancellation, refund, or revocation status. Amounts and currencies are also processed where supplied for verification, reporting, and support. We associate verified access with your WonderLang account so that we can unlock content and restore eligible purchases. Apple or Google handles payment details for in-app purchases; WonderLang does not receive your full payment-card details from those stores. If you purchase through our website, Stripe processes payment details and provides order and payment-status information used to grant access.

Game progress and cloud saves

The app stores game settings and saved progress on your device. When cloud saves are available for your account, it uploads the saved-game files for the selected profile, together with profile names and identifiers, revision information, timestamps, and file-integrity information. These files can contain your character or profile names, language selections, learning progress, quests, game state, playtime, and other information saved by the game. Cloud saves are linked to your WonderLang account and are synchronized with devices using that account and profile. The service maintains the current cloud version and up to three previous revisions for recovery.

Usage and progress information

We process app interactions such as app or game startup, screens viewed, language-pack downloads, tutorial completion, progress checkpoints, free-introduction completion, store or paywall views, purchase-button interactions, and purchase or access-verification results. Associated information can include app version, platform, event time, selected learning and interface languages, language level, playtime, and access tier.

Our own progress-reporting service also receives a randomly generated player identifier stored on the device, the map and progress checkpoint reached, quest counts, character statistics, in-game currency, and selected game settings, including the game's character/grammatical-gender setting. These are game values, not a request for your real-world gender identity. A random identifier can still allow events from the same installation to be associated with one another; it should not be treated as anonymous data.

Device, network, security, and diagnostic information

The app and its service providers process information needed to deliver and protect the service, measure usage, and investigate failures. Depending on the service, this includes IP address, app or installation identifiers, device and operating-system information, app version, network information, integrity or attestation signals, error messages, crash traces, and diagnostic context. IP addresses and SDK information can also be used to infer an approximate country or region. This is different from using the device's precise GPS location.

Advertising-measurement information

We use Meta and TikTok measurement tools to understand whether our advertising leads to app activity, trials, subscriptions, or purchases, and to measure and improve campaigns. They receive relevant event information, such as event type, product, amount, currency, and device, installation, or network information collected by their SDKs. Advertising identifiers can be processed when available to the SDK and permitted by the operating system. Platform and SDK configurations differ; disabling one identifier does not prevent all measurement or all data transmission.

If you use our website to buy or manage access, its conversion-reporting integrations can also send purchase events and matching information, including a hashed email address, IP address, browser information, and advertising click or cookie identifiers when available. Hashing an email address does not make it anonymous. Website-specific cookie and consent notices must explain and control that processing.

Support and reports you choose to send

If you contact us, we process your email address, name if supplied, message, and any screenshots, diagnostic reports, or other information you send. A diagnostic report can contain device information, error details, and game-state context. Please review reports before sending them and avoid including passwords, payment-card details, or information unrelated to your request.

We use security and diagnostic information to prevent fraud and abuse, protect accounts and purchases, detect failures, and improve reliability. Advertising-measurement information helps us evaluate and improve our campaigns. We also retain information where needed to meet applicable legal obligations and handle disputes, refunds, or rights requests.

4. WHO RECEIVES INFORMATION

We disclose information to the providers needed for the purposes described above:

• Google/Firebase: account authentication, account and purchase-access records, cloud-save storage, app integrity where enabled, Firebase/Google Analytics, and Crashlytics.

• Netlify: hosting and operation of the account/API services and progress-reporting endpoint, including necessary network and service logs.

• Apple and Google Play: in-app purchases, subscription and refund handling, purchase verification, content delivery, and platform services.

• Meta and TikTok: app and purchase-event measurement and advertising attribution or campaign improvement.

• Stripe: website payment and subscription processing, where you use that purchase route.

• Email and support-service providers: receiving and responding to support requests and sending service messages. The reviewed backend uses Google Workspace email services. Older website-order fulfillment also uses Google Sheets and MailerLite for order/access delivery; those services can hold related order and contact information if you used that route.

Authorized WonderLang administrators can access account, access-rights, purchase, and save information where needed to operate the service or resolve a support issue. Your cloud saves are not a public social profile.

Some providers act on our instructions; stores, sign-in providers, and advertising partners can also process information for their own purposes under their notices and terms. Information may also be disclosed where required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business transfer, subject to applicable safeguards and notices.

5. YOUR CHOICES AND DEVICE CONTROLS

The game's Options menu includes “Send analytics,” or its localized equivalent. Turning it off stops the game's own progress telemetry from being sent. It does not disable Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Meta, TikTok, essential account or purchase requests, or every diagnostic report.

The current mobile apps do not provide a separate in-app consent control for those SDKs. Device-level advertising or tracking settings can limit certain identifiers and uses, but are not a universal switch that stops all app analytics or network requests. On iOS, any tracking that falls within Apple's App Tracking Transparency rules requires the applicable permission. On Android, advertising-identifier controls depend on the device and Android version.

Signing out does not erase local saved games, and uninstalling the app does not delete your cloud account or cancel a subscription. To request access, correction, objection, or deletion, contact contact@wonderlang.app.

6. RETENTION AND ACCOUNT DELETION

Account and cloud-save information is kept to provide your account and the features you use. Cloud-profile recovery uses the current revision and up to three earlier revisions; it is not a promise that every save is kept for a fixed number of days. Local saves, preferences, cached access information, queued events, and local reports can remain on the device until removed through the app or operating system. Device backup services can retain additional copies under their own settings.

You can request deletion through the WonderLang account page at https://wonderlang.app/account/ using “Request account deletion,” or email contact@wonderlang.app. If a deletion action opens an email draft, you must send that email to submit the request. We may ask you to verify ownership before acting.

The backend's automated deletion process, when available, disables the account and revokes its sessions when a deletion request is confirmed. It schedules permanent removal after a 30-day recovery window. Contact support during that window if you want to cancel the request. After the window, the process removes the account profile, cloud saves, and associated private account records. If automated deletion is unavailable, contact us to arrange deletion and confirm its timing.

A minimized, pseudonymous purchase/access ledger can remain for accounting, refund handling, fraud prevention, and protection against reuse of the same receipt. Pseudonymous records are not necessarily anonymous. Apple, Google, and other providers can retain their own records under their legal obligations and policies. Account deletion does not automatically cancel an Apple or Google Play subscription; manage cancellation in the store where you subscribed.

Provider-side deletion and backup removal can take additional time. For example, Google's Firebase documentation describes a 90-day Crashlytics retention period before removal begins, and up to 180 days for Firebase Authentication data to be removed from live and backup systems after the developer initiates deletion of the user.
