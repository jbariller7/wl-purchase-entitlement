How the adventure helps you learn

Spend more time using your target language because you want to keep playing. Conversations, puzzles, and battles teach and challenge you as you follow the adventure.

  1. 01UnderstandMeet characters. Read and listen, with help when you need it.
  2. 02Reply & practiceForm sentences and play language mini-games.
  3. 03RememberRecall what you learned in spaced-repetition battles.
  4. 04DiscoverConnect clues and use your knowledge to open new paths.

A language-learning RPG: you explore, talk, play, fight, and investigate in the language you’re learning.

Your language. Your next level.

Seven learning languages at launch: French, Latin American Spanish, German, Korean, Japanese, Mandarin Chinese, and English. Every game edition includes all available learning languages and future language updates.

You know the basics. Keep growing.

Teaching starts in late A2 and focuses mainly on B1. A2, B1, and B2 modes adapt the challenges, sentence complexity, grammar tasks, help, and mandatory reviews. Most NPC dialogue stays shared. Return at a higher level for a more demanding replay.

At home or on the go

Planned for PC/Mac, Android, and iOS. Choose Steam or DRM-free on PC/Mac. Multiplatform rewards include all three platforms, unlimited personal mobile devices, and cross-platform cloud saves. Permanent access, no subscription.

Support languages, future languages & level selection

Support text is available in English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese, Korean, Chinese, Japanese, Dutch, Indonesian, Polish, Russian, Ukrainian, and Arabic. Set both languages to your target language for greater immersion.

An early recap and level check help you choose your mode. Japanese and Korean have equivalent JLPT and TOPIK scaling. Complete beginners can build their foundations with the original WonderLang.

Italian and Portuguese can join launch through stretch goals. European Spanish, European and Brazilian Portuguese, Italian, and Russian are also possible later additions where not included at launch; these later releases are not commitments. Language sponsorship tiers are explained in the rewards.

A mystery worth understanding

Your brother Ben is trapped as a beanie. Follow the trail of a missing great-uncle through forests, treetop villages, and jungle paths to find a way to break the curse. No need to have played the first game: an early recap brings you into the story and helps assess your language level.

More proficient language lets us build a deeper story and proper investigations. Talk to people, investigate places, and connect clues across regions. A remark that seemed unimportant might make sense somewhere else. Several regions open early, so you can follow different leads.

Watch how discovery opens new paths

Inspired by Outer Wilds, The Forgotten City, and Chants of Sennaar, while remaining an RPG.

Choose a male, female, or gender-neutral protagonist. Relevant lines adapt to your choice, including voiced NPC dialogue addressing you.

Learn through dialogue

Follow conversations in your target language. Look up unfamiliar words, replay audio, open sentence and grammar explanations, or bookmark a line for later. Then answer by picking words, typing, or speaking aloud.

Your conversation tools, always within reach

Return to encountered words in your dictionary, organize clues in your notepad, and revisit recent lines in the message log.

Vary your practice

Change the pace while practicing vocabulary, grammar, and reading. Crosswords, memory games, conjugation battleship, reading games, board-game sections, word associations, and deduction puzzles give you more reasons to keep using the language.

See returning favorites and new mini-games

Make your learning stick

New words return in spaced-repetition battles, with reviews timed to help you remember. Correct answers power different attacks; your character grows stronger as your language skills grow. Monster shadows move around the map, so you can avoid them or make contact to fight.

A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Before the adventure begins

Do I need to have played the first WonderLang?

No. An early section recaps the original adventure and helps assess your current language level, so you can choose the right challenge.

Which languages will be available at launch?

French, Latin American Spanish, German, Korean, Japanese, Mandarin Chinese, and English are Wave 1. Other languages may follow, as explained in the Story.

Is Beyond designed for complete beginners?

Beyond focuses on A2, B1 and B2 skills. The original WonderLang is designed to help you build the foundations first.

Will I be able to continue on another device?

Cross-platform cloud saves and unlimited personal mobile devices are included from Multiplatform Adventurer (normal €39; VIP €27.30). Single-platform editions let you choose PC/Mac, Android, or iOS.

What does the US$1 payment cover?

It reserves access to the VIP Kickstarter secret reward for your chosen tier, at 30% off its normal price. It is a separate reservation fee, not a game purchase or a Kickstarter pledge. You will make the full VIP pledge separately on Kickstarter after launch. No automatic later charge.

Can I cancel my reservation?

Yes, at any time. Email jonathan@wonderlang.app from the email used at checkout to cancel and request your reservation fee refund.

How will I receive VIP access?

Use an email you can access at Stripe checkout. We record that email and your selected tier with the verified payment, and will use it to provide access to the corresponding secret reward when the Kickstarter launches. The reservation does not subscribe you to a newsletter.

Which currency will I pay in?

The reservation fee is US$1. Stripe may offer an equivalent in your local currency and shows the exact amount before you pay. Reward pledge prices on this page are in euros; the Kickstarter pledge is a separate payment.
