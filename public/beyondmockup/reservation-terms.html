Your VIP reservation

The US$1 fee is separate from the later Kickstarter pledge. It is not deducted from that pledge. Your email and selected tier

Use an email you can access at Stripe checkout. Cancel and request a refund anytime

You can cancel your reservation and request a refund of the reservation fee at any time by emailing jonathan@wonderlang.app. Please contact us from your checkout email and include your payment reference if available. A refunded reservation no longer provides VIP eligibility. Refunds are returned through Stripe to the original payment method; currency conversion and bank processing can affect the local amount and arrival time. If the campaign does not launch, you can request the same refund.

Payment and privacy

Stripe processes the payment and collects your checkout email and payment details. WonderLang keeps a private reservation record containing your email, selected tier and price, payment references, payment amount and currency, date, accepted terms version, and payment or refund status. We do not store card numbers in the reservation database. Only authorized WonderLang administrators can access the records. Your email is used to manage your reservation and provide the promised VIP access, not to automatically subscribe you to a newsletter. We retain the reservation record for administration and applicable accounting obligations. For access, correction, or deletion requests, email jonathan@wonderlang.app.

Advertising measurement

For new VIP reservations, we use Meta’s Conversions API to measure completed US$1 reservations and understand which ads bring people to WonderLang. Where tracking is permitted, we send Meta the verified reservation value, selected tier, a unique payment event reference, a SHA-256 hash of your checkout email, and available ad-click or browser identifiers, IP address, and browser information. We honor Global Privacy Control and your WonderLang marketing opt-out. This does not subscribe you to marketing emails.
